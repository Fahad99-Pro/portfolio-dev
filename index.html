FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does a website cost?
It depends on scope — but every quote is a fixed price agreed before we begin, with no hourly surprises. Tell me about your project and you'll have a clear quote within 24 hours.
How long does a project take?
Most landing pages ship in about a week; business sites in 2–3 weeks; e-commerce stores in 3–4 weeks. I take a limited number of projects at once, so yours never sits in a queue.
How many revisions do I get?
Every project includes revision rounds (1–3 depending on scope). I design in the open — you review at every milestone — so surprises are rare and tweaks stay small.
What do you need from me to start?
Your logo or brand assets if you have them, your content (or I can help with copy direction), and one decision-maker for feedback. That's it — I handle the rest.
Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?
Yes — I work remotely with clients worldwide and communicate in English. Timezone overlap with Europe and most of the US works well.
What happens after launch?
Every project includes launch support, basic SEO setup, and a handover walkthrough so you stay in control. Ongoing care plans (updates, backups, small changes) are available monthly.
